# Plans and billing

## Plans and prices

Tallybook has three plans, billed monthly or yearly:

- **Starter**: $9/month. 1 user, up to 20 invoices per month.
- **Growth**: $29/month. Up to 5 users, unlimited invoices, recurring invoices and automatic payment reminders.
- **Business**: $79/month. Unlimited users, everything in Growth, plus multi-currency invoicing and priority support.

Paying yearly gives two months free (you pay for 10 months). All prices exclude sales tax or VAT, which is added at checkout based on your billing country.

## Changing your plan

Go to **Settings → Billing → Change plan**. Upgrades take effect immediately and you are charged a prorated amount for the rest of the current billing period. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing period; until then you keep the features of your current plan.

If you downgrade to Starter while you have more than one user, the extra users are deactivated at the end of the period. Their invoices stay in your account.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express. Business plan customers paying yearly can also pay by bank transfer; contact support to set this up. We do not accept PayPal or cryptocurrency.

To update your card, go to **Settings → Billing → Payment method**.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, we retry the charge after 3 days and again after 7 days, and email the account owner each time. If the third attempt fails, the account becomes read-only: you can view and download everything, but you can't create or send new invoices until the balance is paid.
