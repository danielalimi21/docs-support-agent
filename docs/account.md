# Account and security

## Adding users

Go to **Settings → Team → Invite user** and enter their email address. Each user can be an **Admin** (full access including billing) or a **Member** (can create and send invoices but can't see billing or delete data). The number of users depends on your plan.

## Two-factor authentication

We strongly recommend turning on two-factor authentication in **Settings → Security**. Tallybook supports authenticator apps (TOTP). SMS codes are not supported.

## Resetting your password

Click **Forgot password** on the login page and we will email you a reset link, valid for 1 hour. If you don't receive it within a few minutes, check your spam folder. If you have lost access to the email address on your account, contact support; we will need to verify your identity before making any change.

## Exporting your data

Go to **Settings → Data → Export**. You can export clients, invoices and payments as CSV, and every invoice as a PDF in a single ZIP file. Exports are available on all plans, including after cancellation while your account is read-only.
