# Refunds and cancellation

## Refund policy

Monthly plans are not refundable, but you can cancel at any time and you won't be charged again.

Yearly plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of the purchase or renewal date. After 30 days, yearly plans are not refundable.

Refunds are reviewed and approved by our billing team; support agents cannot issue them directly. Approved refunds go back to the original card within 5–10 business days.

## Cancelling your subscription

Go to **Settings → Billing → Cancel subscription**. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for, then your account switches to read-only. Your data is kept for 12 months after cancellation, and you can reactivate at any time during that period.

## Deleting your account

Account deletion is permanent and removes all invoices, clients and payment records. Only the account owner can request it, and for security it is handled by our support team after identity verification. We recommend exporting your data first (see **Exporting your data**).
