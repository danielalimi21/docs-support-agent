# Creating and sending invoices

## Creating an invoice

Click **New invoice**, choose a client (or add a new one), and add line items with a description, quantity and unit price. Tallybook calculates line totals, tax and the invoice total automatically. Invoice numbers are assigned in sequence; you can change the prefix and the next number in **Settings → Invoicing → Numbering**.

## Taxes

You can define tax rates in **Settings → Invoicing → Taxes** and apply up to two taxes per line item (for example a state tax and a city tax). Tax can be set as inclusive or exclusive of the line price.

## Sending and reminders

Invoices are emailed to your client from invoices@tallybook.app with your business name as the sender name and your email as the reply-to address. Clients can pay online by card through the link in the email.

On the Growth and Business plans, automatic reminders are sent 3 days before the due date, on the due date, and 7 days after it. You can turn reminders off per client.

## Recurring invoices

Available on Growth and Business. Open an invoice and choose **Make recurring**, then pick weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly. Each new invoice is created and sent automatically on the schedule. Recurring invoices can be paused or stopped at any time from **Invoices → Recurring**.

## Multi-currency

Available on the Business plan only. You can invoice a client in any of 40 supported currencies; set the currency on the client profile. Reports convert amounts to your home currency using the exchange rate on the invoice date.
